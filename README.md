**Solmar Heat** — a 2D top-down open-world crime game set in the fictional sun-soaked coastal city of Solmar Bay. You are Leo Marlow, a driver building a crew and taking the bay from the Viper gang while the Solmar Bay Police Department hunts you. Explore the city on foot and by car, steal cars, run missions, outrun the police, and climb the wanted ladder. Built with Phaser 3 (vendored) from static files — no network requests, no downloads.

Features: seeded procedural city (roads, districts, beach, downtown), driving with traffic and pedestrians, car stealing, a wanted level with police who patrol, chase and search, 6 story missions, on-foot combat, HUD with minimap, start/pause/save, and synthesized WebAudio sound and music.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Keyboard (WASD/arrows to move and drive, E to enter/exit cars, Space to attack, mouse to aim and shoot) and mouse.
